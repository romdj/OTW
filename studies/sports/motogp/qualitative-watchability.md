# Qualitative Racing Watchability

## Interpretation

Expected-order, pole-to-flag dominance usually has low uncertainty and state
change, but an unchanged order can remain tense when riders are within credible
attack range, tyre performance is converging or the championship is live. Report
independent dimensions—battle pressure, lead uncertainty, meaningful position
change, pace/tyre branching, upset/recovery, execution, flow and stakes—before any
viewer-weighted summary.

In MotoGP, a position change means a persistent on-track order change between
riders on the same racing lap. Exclude pit-lane movement, timing corrections,
yellow/red-flag intervals and immediate back-and-forth noise unless the exchange
itself is verified. Expected order is a pre-session rider/bike/circuit distribution,
not factory status. Mechanical failures add disruption and narrative, not skill.
Crashes, injury and danger never add value. Weather and flags matter only through
changed options/uncertainty; repeated stoppages reduce flow.

## Evidence by data grade

- **Classification (≤.40 confidence):** grid/final order, status, gap, laps. Supports
  only coarse expected-order deviation and persistent net gains.
- **Lap timing (≤.75):** order/gaps each sector/lap, tyre, flags, weather and race
  control. Supports closing pressure, stable passes, lead-path uncertainty, pace
  reversals, group fragmentation and restart-adjusted flow.
- **Tracking/video (≤.90):** position traces and reviewed footage support corner-
  sequence exchanges, line choice, braking/defensive execution and causal labels.

Missing cause is `UNKNOWN`, not an overtake. Return every dimension with coverage,
confidence, cohort and model version; do not rank sparse final-only races beside
fully observed races without grade-specific calibration.

## Calibration, safety and disclosure

At least three trained viewers annotate full races in short windows and overall,
separating pressure, execution and enjoyment. Sample Sprint/GP, wet/dry, factories/
independents, circuits and eras; hold out rounds/providers. Measure agreement,
format regret and errors caused by famous riders, raw pass totals or attrition.

Strict mode exposes only pre-start importance. It hides all observed dimensions,
grid surprise, changes, weather, flags, failures, duration and viewing format. After
reveal use evidence phrases without winner/crash detail unless authorized. The
[FIM Grand Prix regulations](https://www.fim-moto.com/fileadmin/user_upload/Documents/2026/FIM_2026_MotoGP__Moto2___Moto3_World_Championship_Regulations.pdf)
define classifications, interruptions and restarts; the qualitative mapping is an
OTW hypothesis, calibrated from full-event viewing rather than highlights.
