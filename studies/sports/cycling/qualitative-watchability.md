# Qualitative Racing Watchability

## Position depends on the contest

Cycling has no single “position change.” Road position inside a group, group order,
stage virtual lead, general classification, points/mountains contests and cyclocross
lap order are distinct states. Expected order must likewise be frozen per objective
from roster, route, role and form; a domestique finishing outside the top ten is not
necessarily underperformance.

Describe `outcomeUncertainty`, `groupTacticalChange`, `attackViability`,
`classificationThreat`, `underdogUpset`, `execution`, `flow`, and `stakes` before
forming a personalized score. A predicted favourite controlling to the expected
finish generally offers little state change, but an apparently dominant team can
remain under pressure from a viable break, isolation, crosswind or GC leverage.
Weather can create tactical branches; repeated stoppages/neutralizations reduce
flow. Mechanical failure is disruption, never skill. Crashes, injury, dangerous
roads and harm contribute zero entertainment value.

## Data grades

- **Results/checkpoints (confidence ≤.40):** coarse final surprise, gaps and
  classification change; cannot infer attacks or team control.
- **Timed race state (≤.72):** persistent groups/composition/gaps, terrain, race-
  radio events, classifications and weather support catch probability, bridges,
  selections, isolation, responsibility shifts and virtual classifications.
- **Tracking/power/video (≤.88):** road positioning, drafting context, team movement,
  attack effort, technical CX/descending execution and causal review. GPS jitter,
  missing transponders and selective broadcast visibility remain explicit.

Use persistence windows and tag cause. A move scores only its change to credible
outcome paths, not its count. Grade and calibrate road one-day, stage, TT and
cyclocross separately; missing race-state data is unknown, not low quality.

## MOCK contrasts

- `source=MOCK`: Amber Stage—favourite team controls a five-minute doomed break and
  delivers the forecast sprint: high execution, low uncertainty until highlights.
- `source=MOCK`: Ridge Classic—unfavoured two-rider move remains catchable while
  favourites split responsibility; lead probability swings without any crash: full.
- `source=MOCK`: Frost Cyclocross—two riders exchange a stable lap lead through
  technical sections; a neutralized section interrupts rhythm: tension high, flow
  reduced, safety event not rewarded.

## Calibration and disclosure

At least three raters annotate full available coverage in terrain/time windows and
separately label tactical quality, uncertainty, execution, flow and format. Sample
women's/men's fields, event tiers, route types, break outcomes, CX conditions and
feed grades; hold out races/providers. Audit fame bias, futile-attack inflation,
crash-driven selection and broadcast-selection bias.

Strict mode exposes only pre-start importance. Groups, gaps, attacks, realized
weather, failures, neutralizations, duration, dimensions and format are sealed.
Revealed explanations can say “a viable move repeatedly changed the chase” without
naming riders/results until authorized. The [UCI regulations index](https://www.uci.org/regulations/3MyLDDrwJCJJ0BGGOFzOat)
defines discipline classifications and race procedures; OTW's qualitative mapping
must be calibrated with full-event raters rather than highlight edits.
