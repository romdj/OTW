# Qualitative Racing Watchability

## Discipline-aware dimensions

Score descriptions before preference: `battlePressure`, `leadUncertainty`,
`meaningfulOrderChange`, `paceOrTyreBranching`, `upsetRecovery`, `execution`,
`flow`, and `weekendStakes`. Pole-to-flag expected-order dominance is normally low
in uncertainty/change, yet close pressure or Race 2/championship leverage can keep
it tense. Calibrate Race 1, the shorter Superpole Race and Race 2 separately.

A meaningful change is stable same-lap on-track order, not a timing correction,
lapping, pit passage or transient sample. Expected order is frozen before that
session and accounts for rider/bike/circuit form plus the weekend's grid rules; do
not equate factory status with merit. Failures are disruption, not skill. Crashes,
injury and dangerous severity are never rewards. A flag or wet change may open
competitive branches, but repeated stoppages reduce flow and restart bunching is
not organic pressure.

## Data grades

- **Final/grid only (confidence ≤.40):** coarse persistent net gain, finish spread
  and expected-order deviation; no battle or strategy claims.
- **Lap/sector timing (≤.75):** stable order/gaps, groups, lead probability, pace
  crossovers, flags/restarts and condition windows. Attribute changes and discount
  neutralized intervals.
- **Tracking plus reviewed video (≤.90):** corner-level exchanges, alternative
  lines, defensive quality, braking execution and contact-free causal attribution.

Return per-dimension value, interval, coverage, missing causes and rules/model
version. Unknown incident attribution remains unknown. Never impute missing timing
as a dull race or compare short/full races against one raw-change threshold.

## Annotation and spoiler boundary

Three or more raters annotate complete sessions in lap windows, separately judging
pressure, skill, flow and preferred format. Stratify by session type, circuit,
weather, era and team prominence; hold out rounds/providers and audit false pass
counts, attrition-led upsets and brand bias. Agreement limits claimed precision.

Protected clients receive pre-session importance only. Observed order changes,
flags, weather, failures, duration, dimensions and format remain sealed. Revealed
copy may say “persistent multi-rider pressure” without naming the result; incident
detail follows the user's spoiler level. The versioned
[FIM WorldSBK regulations](https://www.fim-moto.com/fileadmin/user_upload/Documents/2026/2026_FIM_WorldSBK_WorldSSP_WorldSPB_WorldWCR_World_Championship_Regulations_CLEAN_FINAL.pdf)
govern grids, classifications, interruptions and restarts; OTW's watchability
interpretation remains a full-session calibration hypothesis.
